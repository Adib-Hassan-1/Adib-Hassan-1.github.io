title: High-Performance Dual-Channel PCF-SPR  Biosensor for Broad-Range
  Multi-Analyte  Biomedical Sensing
summary: |-
  This paper proposes a dual-core, dual-channel 
  Photonic crystal fiber-based surface plasmon resonance (PCF
  SPR) biosensor, numerically evaluated via the finite element 
  method (FEM), and capable of simultaneous multi-analyte 
  detection over a refractive index (RI) range of 1.18-1.42 using 
  gold as the plasmonic layer. The proposed design has maximum 
  wavelength sensitivity of 38000nm/RIU, amplitude sensitivity of 
  1414.43 RIU−1, figure of merit of 655.17 RIU−1 and sensor 
  resolution of 9×10−6 RIU. There exists no crosstalk between the 
  two channels and the structural tolerance analysis shows that 
  the design is robust under reasonable fabrication deviations. In 
  addition to RI sensing, the sensor is able to discriminate six 
  cancer cell types, with a maximum sensitivity of 7142.85 nm/RIU 
  for MCF-7 cells, and four bacterial pathogens, measuring up to 
  4878.04 nm/RIU for Enterococcus faecalis. These findings 
  highlight its potential for practical diagnostic applications.
