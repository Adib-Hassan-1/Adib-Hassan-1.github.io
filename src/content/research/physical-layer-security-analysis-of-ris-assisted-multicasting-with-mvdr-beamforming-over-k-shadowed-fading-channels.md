title: Physical Layer Security Analysis of RIS-Assisted Multicasting with MVDR
  Beamforming Over κ-µ Shadowed Fading Channels
summary: >-
  This paper presents a physical layer security analysis of RIS-assisted
  multicasting with minimum variance dis

  tortionless response (MVDR) beamforming over κ-µ shadowed fading channels. The
  MVDR framework is applied at the RIS to jointly maximize the received SINR at
  the weakest legitimate destination user and suppress the signal leakage toward
  a passive eavesdropper. By treating the cascade RIS channel as a virtual
  array, the MVDR weight vector is derived in closed form via the
  Sherman–Morrison identity, and the resulting SINR is shown to follow a κ-µ
  shadowed distribution through moment-matching.

  Leveraging this statistical characterization, closed-form expres

  sions are derived for three security metrics applicable to K

  user multicast scenarios: the ergodic secrecy capacity (ESC), the

  probability of non-zero secrecy capacity (PNZSC), and the secure

  outage probability (SOP). Monte Carlo simulations validate the

  analytical results, and numerical evaluations demonstrate that

  increasing the number of RIS elements N, the Rician factor κ,

  and the multipath clustering parameter µ consistently improves

  secrecy performance. Results further confirm that MVDR beam

  forming provides a measurable security gain over conventional

  coherent phase alignment, particularly when the eavesdropper

  channel is strong.
