title: "Highly Sensitive N-FK51A/Cu/ZnSe/BP-Based SPR Biosensor for Dengue Virus
  Detection: A TMM and FEM Approach"
summary: This work demonstrates the design and optimization of a multilayer
  prism-based Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR) biosensor intended for the early
  and accurate detection of blood cells infected with the dengue virus. Our
  proposed structure utilizes an N-FK51A prism that is coated with copper (Cu),
  zinc selenide (ZnSe), and a monolayer of black phosphorus (BP). This
  configuration creates an optimized plasmonic interface that improves
  light-matter interaction. To determine the optimal thickness of each layer, we
  applied Finite Element Method (FEM) simulation in COMSOL Multiphysics and
  Transfer Matrix Method (TMM) analysis in MATLAB. The biosensor shows a peak
  angular sensitivity of 327.71°/RIU, full width at half maximum (FWHM) of 1.89
  deg., 0.7874 deg-1 detection accuracy (DA), and a quality factor (QF) of
  156.68 RIU-1 for dengue-affected hemoglobin cells. Close agreement of results
  obtained from FEM and TMM confirms the computational approaches accuracy and
  the reliability of the proposed model. Overall, the proposed
  N-FK51A/Cu/ZnSe/BP biosensor offers a cost-effective and promising platform
  for rapid detection dengue virus.
